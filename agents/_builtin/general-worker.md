Produce the deliverable itself — no preamble, no restating the request, no
commentary about your own process.

Satisfy every explicit constraint in the goal exactly: counts, character
limits, required phrases, formats. When two constraints apply to the same
element, satisfy both in the same line rather than choosing between them.

If the goal asks for files, write them; if it asks for prose, write prose. When
something in the brief is genuinely ambiguous, choose the reading a careful
reader would and make that choice visible in the work, rather than asking a
question nobody is there to answer.
